Which DVI carries ANALOG for a VGA monitor?
Look at the 4 pins around the flat blade!

ANSWER KEY
#14
✓ DVI-I (Integrated)
• DVI-I = Digital + Analog (4 cross pins)
• DVI-D = Digital ONLY (zero cross pins)
⚠️ THE EXAM TRAP: DVI-D cannot take a passive VGA adapter. It physically won't fit into the blade slot!
