DVI-I (Integrated)
- The 4 analog pins around the flat blade carry the VGA signal.
- If it only has the flat blade and no 4 pins, it is DVI-D (digital only).
Exam trap: DVI-A is analog only. DVI-D is digital only. DVI-I integrates both.
