Every key term the Exam 2 study guide and review deck point to, sorted into the four rubber bands and turned into cards: 185 terms, 137 cards. The cards come from Bidgoli Modules 3, 9, 4, 5 and 6, Knaflic Chapter 1, and the CRISP-DM and Power BI lectures. Definitions follow the course glossary and the class slides; nothing here is a rival definition.

Each band asks for a different kind of study, so each gets its own deck.

Every card works the same way: click the card or press Space to flip it, and use the arrow keys or the buttons to move. Shuffle mixes the deck. The sort sheet at the bottom lists every term with its band, which is a good start on your one-page note sheet.
