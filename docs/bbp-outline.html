Audience: course authors, and anyone handing the kit to an LLM

TITLE
Make Terms Make Sense Through Story
Weave a set of terms and concepts into one story that shows how they connect.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You have a module, exam section, or lesson full of terms to teach. You're an author who wants learners to understand how the ideas depend on each other.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Definitions stay floating. Learners can repeat terms but can't connect them. Write a brief, draft and validate the narrative, then publish it. Lessons where learners connect the terms, not just repeat them.
Transition 1A story supplies the connection. Three ideas explain the kit: why story, how the files work, and how an LLM can help.
