*Status: resolved, 2026-10-26. Every item below was decided by Charles and applied on `main`. This file is now a record; file paths in the tables are as they were at audit time, and several files have since moved to `archive/`.*

## Scope and method

This audit covers the rule-bearing documents: the root files, `schema/`, `template/`, `examples/PORTLAND_DESK.md`, `research/`, `docs/research/`, `tools/` docs, and `portland-desk/README.md`. It does not audit chapter prose or workbench content line by line; PR #29 does that for chapters 1 and 2.

Two decisions are already settled and are not re-flagged here. First, the learner stays safe and gets the satisfaction in the workbench, while Ethan takes real but realistic costs; "satisfying conclusion" is not a rule (PR #30, `docs/craft/learning-narrative-framework.md`). Second, canon: Harrowmere Equipment Group, Portland, and Ethan never relocates.

## What this PR changes

1. Adds a rule near the top of `AGENTS.md` telling agents to ignore `archive/`.
2. Adds `archive/README.md`, which says the folder is superseded and lists what moved.
3. Moves two explicit version 1 reviews into `archive/docs-research/` with `git mv`. Nothing else moved and nothing was deleted. No file in the repo linked to either one.
4. No safe typo fixes were needed. A typo and doubled-word scan of the rule-bearing documents found none.
