What "verified" means here:

- **Verified** means the bibliographic record (authors, year, title, venue, volume, pages) was confirmed this session against Crossref, arXiv, ERIC, Open Library, ADS, or the publisher's own page, and the DOI or URL resolves. It does **not** mean the full text was read. Effect sizes quoted below come from published abstracts.
- **Unverified** means the record could not be confirmed this session and is given from memory. Check it before citing.

The "Named in doc?" column says whether the document names the work or idea directly (**Named**), names the concept but not the source (**Concept**), or does not mention it and it is added because it tests or qualifies a claim (**Added**).

## Claims in the document with no source found

These are stated as rules in the document. No primary research supporting the specific claim was found this session. Treat them as practitioner heuristics.

- The **10 / 25 / 15** block and its 7 / 8 / 10 sub-splits. Do not justify this with a 10-minute attention span; #44 and #45 find little support for that claim. Active learning beating lecture is supported by #46, but the exact minute counts are not. As of 2026-11-11, treat 10/25/15 as a per-hour guide based on task sizing (an explain-and-demonstrate opening small enough for a novice to try next, because experts underestimate novice time), not as a rule. See [errata.md](errata.md).
