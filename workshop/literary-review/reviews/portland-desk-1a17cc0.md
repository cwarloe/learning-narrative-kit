## Verdict

The opening has acquired a narrower score, better tool provenance, and later consequences, but its central causal claim still reads as overnight transformation. Across the book, the problem is uneven: early competence and some mentor-directed diagnoses resolve too readily; later chapters contain credible teamwork, consequential mistakes, incomplete repairs, and human resistance. This needs selective structural revision, not another blanket layer of injuries or penalties.

## Ranked findings

### 1. Highest priority: the study-method turnaround still has no practice interval

**Observed text.** Six weeks of copying definitions leaves Ethan at 61% on the last practice test; the retake is eight hours away (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/00-the-four-rubber-bands.md:15`, `:17`, `:34`). He then understands his previous failure, sorts the material, and spends the next hour making three field slates (`:60`, `:105`). At the exam, the new frameworks “took over”; he isolates faults and eliminates impossibilities (`:167`, `:169`). Even the router simulation that initially consumes eight minutes receives a “clean configuration” before submission (`:163`, `:171`). The 690 pass is genuinely narrow (`:175`).

**Interpretation.** Prior study plausibly supplied facts, and better organization can help immediately. What remains unearned is the story's confidence that one insight produced a usable diagnostic reflex under pressure. The score reduction changes the margin of victory, not the mechanism of learning. A night of arranging tools establishes ownership, not fluency. There is no intervening attempt that tests the new method, exposes a limitation, or requires adaptation.

**Minimal direction.** Make the pass rest chiefly on knowledge he had already accumulated; present the bands as an unfinished way of organizing it. Change the exam's certainty into bounded use: the method helps him reason through some items and leaves others unresolved. Do not merely add another anxious paragraph.

**Author decision required:** either retain the next-morning 690 canon and substantially reduce what the new strategy accomplishes, or create an actual multi-day/multi-week practice interval and synchronize the affected timeline/bible descriptions. One more late-night practice score alone would not establish weeks of skill development.

### 2. High: the father still functions more as a delivery system than an opposing person

**Observed text.** Ethan objects that he must memorize an impossible quantity of material (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/00-the-four-rubber-bands.md:34`). His father supplies Hank's example, an electronics diagnosis, and the machine-shop/catalog analogy, then leaves (`:38`, `:44`, `:48`, `:50`). Ethan's mature taxonomy follows directly. The forum templates were found earlier (`:105`), but their usefulness activates perfectly after this conversation.

**Interpretation.** The father does not have to be ignorant or wrong. The missing resistance is between two people who want something: Ethan wants reassurance or a way out; his father supplies the chapter's correct frame without having to accommodate Ethan's valid concern about arbitrary exam facts. The scene supplies wisdom rather than negotiating its limits. More sources of the same wisdom do not cure that mechanism.

**Minimal direction.** Keep the glass, the tired parent, and the useful mechanical analogy. Let Ethan's concrete objection change the conversation: the father can help him inspect mechanisms, while conceding that this does not solve exam trivia or tomorrow's deadline. Give the relationship a small subsequent action or unanswered need, rather than another speech. Avoid inventing a family quarrel or requiring the father to give false advice.

### 3. High: Episode 01 exceeds the pre-rotation capability established for Ethan

**Observed text.** Rosa's correction makes the meter sequence credible (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/01-the-paper-cert.md:73`, `:87`). But after a delicate pin repair, Ethan identifies a specific power-loss reset from 19,200 to 9,600 baud before showing a source for those settings (`:118`, `:122`, `:134`, `:136`). He then calculates bit times, interprets the exact divider switches, changes them, and restarts a daemon (`:140`, `:144`, `:146`). This happens before his IT rotation, not after it (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/02-the-live-board.md:7`).

**Interpretation.** The chapter now earns more of its physical work, and repeated failed fixes create real pressure. It still concentrates hardware diagnosis, fine connector work, industrial serial configuration, and command-line operation in a freight worker's one night, beyond his limited established practical experience. “Neither of us knew what came next” (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/01-the-paper-cert.md:105`) is followed by highly specific knowledge rather than its acquisition. The late trailer is a real consequence; it cannot establish the missing competence retroactively.

**Minimal direction.** Keep Ethan's useful observation and contribution, but give each unfamiliar serial fact an observable source and a verification step. Reduce his independent work to a smaller plausible contribution, or establish a prior bounded encounter with this controller. Another person can legitimately complete the specialist part; do not expand Rosa's expertise to cover everything or introduce a perfectly timed all-knowing rescuer. Changing his approved backstory requires an author decision.

### 4. High: several diagnoses belong to the mentor, while Ethan performs the answer

**Observed text.** In Episode 03 Jules names APIPA and the trunk mechanism, directs subnet arithmetic step by step, then directs Ethan to the ARP evidence (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/03-the-default-gateway-doesnt-care.md:43`, `:49`, `:86`, `:103`, `:123`). In Episode 04 Lena identifies the VPN-profile mistake immediately, Jules gives the complete mechanism, and Ethan subsequently “takes the evidence” to Lena, who is already beside him explaining it (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/04-the-name-is-not-the-number.md:108`, `:110`, `:118`). In Episode 05 Jules points him to the destination process and later the source workstation, then provides the pooling solution (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/05-the-wire-has-two-ends.md:62`, `:121`, `:166`).

**Interpretation.** These are useful explanations and legitimate team interventions. Their cumulative arrangement makes Ethan's main contribution compliance with the correct adult's instructions. The reader experiences a guided demonstration more often than uncertain judgment. Episode 04's duplicated escalation also obscures the moment when his evidence changes somebody else's action.

**Minimal direction.** In one substantial beat per affected chapter, let Ethan choose the next discriminating observation, discover what that observation cannot establish, or decide when escalation is warranted. Keep senior-owned configuration changes with the seniors. In Episode 04 combine the diagnosis and handoff into one clear decision. The goal is visible responsibility, not junior omniscience or mentors pretending ignorance.

Jared particularly needs his real expertise protected. He blames a carrier in Episode 04 (line 24) and a firewall/server repeatedly in Episode 05 (lines 9, 58, and 117), while Jules knows the correction. His point that firewalls can generate resets is reasonable (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/05-the-wire-has-two-ends.md:60`); let it constrain the test instead of being brushed aside. His successful repairs in Episodes 03 and 06 and his first discovery of the common jump host in Episode 12 are valuable counterweights.

### 5. Medium: some protocol/identity queues resolve negotiation when the right term appears

**Observed text.** Jared agrees to SSH after the reminder of last year's capture and returns a working login eleven minutes later (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/07-the-port-that-is-open.md:32`, `:36`, `:40`). Maya changes her mail profile after a protocol explanation (same file, lines 50, 58, and 60). Sam initially grumbles at the jump host but then declares it preferable; the remaining queue receives the “secure twin” in a paragraph (same file, lines 122 and 124). In Episode 08 Todd accepts the MFA correction and enrollment quickly (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/08-prove-who-you-are.md:50`, `:56`, `:68`).

**Interpretation.** Fast routine fixes are believable and give the book useful variation. The cumulative pattern, especially Episode 07's breadth, lets correct explanation do most of the persuasive work. A person's actual cost—lost access, unavailable device, missed work—is often less present during the decision than the vocabulary being taught. Dialogue can sound like questions arranged to introduce the next definition.

**Important limits.** Episode 07 collects the patch's consequences in its Saturday outage. Episode 08 explicitly leaves Nora's manager installation pending (line 38), keeps Dev's script stopped (line 86), and charges Ethan for the manager's tone complaint (lines 114, 116, and 118). Episode 09's Marisol verifies independently and AP absorbs a real batch delay (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/09-dont-click-that.md:58`, `:92`). These resistances are already present; do not erase them in describing this problem.

**Minimal direction.** Choose one central negotiation to give more room, and shorten a lesser vignette to pay for it. Let a reasonable operational objection alter implementation or timing, while preserving the security boundary. There is no requirement that every caller argue, every brief ticket become a full scene, or every fix fail.

### 6. High: Episode 12 turns supporting evidence into a fully known attack history

**Observed text.** The chapter establishes an anomalous login, malicious mailbox actions, encoded PowerShell/LSASS telemetry, and periodic outbound traffic (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/12-when-the-ticket-escalates.md:52`, `:54`, `:66`, `:68`). Ethan then states that credentials were phished the previous night, MFA was bypassed using an adversary-in-the-middle proxy, and a memory payload was dropped that morning (line 70). Those specific entry-mechanism claims have no corresponding observation shown. Later the shared jump host and matching external destination support a serious linked incident (lines 82, 86, and 88), but Ethan calls the whole intrusion synchronized with “terrifying clarity” (line 90).

**Interpretation.** The story author knows the designed chain; Ethan is receiving evidence. Blurring those horizons gives him heroic certainty despite appropriate team escalation. A strong responder can identify immediate danger without knowing initial access or proving one coordinated attacker. His ability to say what remains unknown would mark growth.

**Minimal direction.** Separate observed facts, working hypotheses, and what CSIRT must establish. Either supply the missing entry evidence or qualify the specific phishing/AiTM account. This need not delay isolation. Likewise, make explicit how April's wireless baseline affects this incident: the current opening asserts that connection (line 9), but the decisive evidence shown is identity/mailbox/host telemetry. Do not imply merely saving data caused an unrelated discovery.

### 7. Medium: the capstone replays the first lesson without establishing the experienced Ethan's regression

**Observed text.** In Episode 10 Ethan recognizes Maya's bundled adware, removes it, and keeps the ticket open over the approved replacement (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/10-something-on-the-box.md:48`, `:54`, `:64`). Two months later Maya presents similar ads/slowness; he reaches for imaging before looking at history, and Jules again supplies the interpretation (`docs/portland-desk/narrative/12-when-the-ticket-escalates.md:24`, `:26`, `:32`, `:40`). He calls himself an apprentice again (line 42).

**Interpretation.** Persistent panic is a valid standing struggle. Repeating a mistake is not automatically a defect. Here the experienced response disappears primarily so the capstone can reteach event versus incident. Readers can see alternating incompetence and spectacular insight rather than a recognizable technician whose old weakness emerges under a particular pressure.

**Minimal direction.** Give the repeated reflex a specific present trigger, or let Ethan make the initial sensible adware assessment while Jules corrects a more advanced inference. Preserve the mentor and contrast between noisy harmless activity and quiet compromise. Make the error reflect this stage of his career rather than resetting him to Episode 02.

## Smallest useful revision sequence

1. Decide Episode 00's time constraint versus the amount of new learning it claims. This is the foundational author decision.
2. Bound Episode 01's independent serial competence and show where indispensable facts come from.
3. Give Ethan one consequential investigative choice in Episodes 03–05; retain the team's authority and explanatory clarity. Preserve Jared's reasonable technical objection and demonstrated skill.
4. Develop one meaningful user negotiation rather than enlarging every vignette.
5. Qualify Episode 12's unproved attack history and distinguish its experienced Ethan from the beginner in Episode 02.

Judge the revised text by changed causal behavior: what did Ethan try, what resisted, what evidence changed his next action, and what could he still not do? Another lower score, missed lunch, scraped knuckle, or reprimand cannot answer those questions by itself. Existing later episodes already supply models for credible difficulty; the entire series does not need replacement.
